# Golden Scenario Catalog (v1 Parity)

1. Opening roll allows only piece types with legal moves.
2. Dice forces knight move when only knight has legal move.
3. Castling denied if king passes through attacked square.
4. Castling allowed when all conditions pass.
5. En passant available exactly one reply turn.
6. Promotion flow on reaching final rank.
7. In-check filtering removes pseudo-legal moves.
8. Checkmate detection when no legal moves and in check.
9. Stalemate detection when no legal moves and not in check.
10. Event replay reproduces final state byte-for-byte.
